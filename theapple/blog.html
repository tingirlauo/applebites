november 8th 2026 . . .

Trigger warning for senstive topics, e.i eating disorders . Hello, I wanted to write up a little something here, since updates have been coming at a very slow pace, and seem to have stooped to a full halt as of late. I don't mean for it-- I want more than anything to put my focus back onto my site and teaching myself code, but I've been going through a lot lately that has made it very challenging to do the things that I wish to do. Not to mention the things that I must do, for that matter. I have a portfolio deadline that has hung over me like a dreadful promise for about three months now, and I haven't had the chance to get to it cause of... well, a lot. Maybe half of it are excuses, or so my mind says. I tried looking for a job and was quite actively consumed by the hunt for a few weeks, did interviews and even a trial day at a bistro restaurant, but none of it worked out. Some of the places I reached out to even played right in my face (cough, the restaurant..) and I came out of that with 6 hours of my life spent working hands and knees, doing manual labour for the benefit of another, and not getting a dime out of it-- just a free pizza. I decided about a week and a half ago that I was giving up my search for pay to actually focus on my projects, even with the ever looming financial crisis blossoming in my home. But I've found myself mentally blocked even then. Not just out of the stress in my home, but for a multitude of things. My sister is suffering of an eating disorder, and it's been especially hard for my family to navigate. I've been reading up a lot and trying my best to educate myself on Anorexia Nervosa, and have found that it really does rewire the way your brain thinks and even, warps the body's hunger signals, increases taste sensitivity as the result of such an extended period of restrictive eating habits. Even eating can cause discomfort, so I've heard, and it's hard to help someone with it when you yourself have no clue how its like. She is so young too, and I mean, she's turning into an official teen only just this late November. I want so desperately for her to love herself, and I've been trying to challenge her beliefs in small ways. Trying to teach her how to speak to her self-image kindly, and to not be sucked into the dangerous lie of aesthetics. (I hate to think too little too late, but we are working on it) Only a year ago it wasn't like this, but now that it is I can't sit and linger or sulk on why but only be there for her the best I can. I'm trying, i'm trying to be there for everyone in my family, each of whom is taking it differently. But I can't be there for eveyone and myself too. Or at least I find it very hard to. I'm the eldest sister, and I feel like.. a lot of things. A lot expectaions that i'm trying to reach, but feel i have so little time and space to get there. Even a trip I had originally planned for december, I fear I won't be in the right headspace for it when it comes because of all that has overwhelming me thus far. And it sucks it really does. I feel drained, mentally, and in a state of, just, shitty. I'm not the role model I should be- should've been long ago. I want to provide, and I want to thrive creatively cause fuck thats what I've always wanted and yet, I can't fight the exhaustion. I want to and frankly need to create cause the deadlines are creeping and crawling up and I feel like a deer caught in the headlights. I talk a lot with each of my Family members to try and break down how they are feeling so no one feels alone in their frustration with everything and so everyone feels seen and heard at least to some extent so that we can all at the very least be on the same page to push past all these difficulties together. But it takes a toll on you, and it takes the time away from you too. I can complain about a million different things and I've told myself very often that I just need to rise against the odds. Like my grandma says, ocupate, no te preocupes. I wonder how much of it is true. I would like to just, i don't know. But I want to get things done I really do. This site, my art, my writing, I yearn to create and I hunger to make content but it's just not coming out of me right now and I feel guilty about it. And i'm tiered often times. I've been sleeping really late lately. It doesnt help that I share my room with my brother, so him waking up before me when I have no need to go anywhere always wakes me. I know I will get out of this, I dont want to complain to anyone that only sees me as a person to get something fun and exciting out of, so I just kinda end up.. self isolating. And ough, I want a different rhythm. And I hope those people will be there until I'm ready to be myself again. I know they will be but who will be real with me in the meawhile? What a sore, Holy rant. Maybe someone relates idk, if you read this far uh golden star for hearing out on my problems. But no really, I will come back to all of this when I can. I really want this, even if others may not.

october 10th 2026 . . .

Hey so can I just be honest real quick? I don't know what in the ever living fuck i'm going to be writing out on here. How much of myself can i give to strangers before i hit a mark that is too much? Will anyone hear me if i scream out into the abyss that is the black hole of the internet? Certainly it shouldn't be something I fear. What I write of myself here, I have no idea of telling if it speaks to the viewer, whoever that may be. I'm a very introspective person by nature, and I tend to think a lot about things. And I mean oh boy, a lot. I know some things should remain private, and be reserved for pen and paper, stuffed into some old cabinet so that it may never again see the light of day. But out on here? It's imprinted on the digital sphere for all of eternity. I don't know if that's the wisest thing to do, but then again, what else is there to keep a record of me? I tend to think about people of the past, the millions of stories that remain eternally untold simply cause they did not have the means to document their lives. Their thoughts, their ways of life, the memories they laid to rest at their graves. Let's not get sappy now, but life is short. Isn't that what they all say? Live it to the fullest, live it now, for you may never know when it passes you by. I'm often afraid of judgement, of saying the wrong thing, of upsetting someone by the slightest mishap. Tone it down, reread, and by the grace of god, don't offend. Why do we spend our whole lives filtering ourselves out? Such a large fraction of our thoughts is seized by hesitation, of a keen awareness of the self. It's both a blessing and a curse. We become distinct versions of ourselves, each catered to another person or another group of people. But in this process of dismantling and cataloging, what happens to us? The real us that sits beneath it all? Does she erode for the stranger? Does she pick herself clean so that they may see her as a smooth and smothered husk of what she really is? Am I really to live the rest of my life trying to make myself more palatable for those who don't know me? Or shall I lay myself bare as I am? Will you savour me then?

october 5th 2026 . . .

If there's anything you should know about me, it's that i'm ambitious as fuck. And i know this applies to various individuals, but I especially can't help but live with this nagging feeling over my shoulders, and it's there from the moment i wake up. I want to learn how to code to build my site, so i'm urged to get up and turn on that laptop and get on it from the second my day starts. I'm urged to improve my craft and get fucking good at art, cause if i'm mediocore then its never enough, is it? I'm eager to educate myself, to learn so that i may create with taste. I feel like i lack it now. I feel like as a dropout, essentially, I don't have what it takes to be good now. I didn't go to any fancy art school, or anything of the sort. I'll only have it, that thing people spend their whole lives chasing, if i focus, and get good. Or so I tell myself. It's the least i can do isn't it? But then perfectionism ruins it. I cannot create if i feel it must be good, because if its bad then it can't be shared, right? This way of thinking harms the creative in me that hungers for space, because it assures she never gets to pick up the pen in the first place, or open the damn google doc and type out the story that has picked up dust in her spinning head for so long. I love my characters like a mother loves her children, and I want to give them everything. The art, the edits, the platform to exist on, the site, the story, the damn pinterest boards (done and given... will remake inevitably). And maybe one day when i have it all, i can give them to the public. But if its not perfect as I imagine? Then is it fruitless? Or is it better to have something out there than to have nothing at all that exists in the realm of the physical. I get so afraid thinking these characters will die with me, die in my head. I would, as many artists do, give everything to share my stories with the world, and have people connect with what i create. I am eternally grateful that i can at the very least share my love for them with a few select people, as many of the stories i cherish are shared narratives. these are people i've known for years, and who still sometimes message me and god i love them to death. I know i'm lucky to have this, and i guess it motivates me more so to push forward. To create if not for myself, then for them. I'm an ambitious mf, and one day i'll get to where i dream of being. I know you can too. in fact, why don't we do it together? Stick around for more, so much more is to come. Thank you.
